Let's think step by step to solve this problem.

1. **Understand the Indian National Calendar**: The Indian National Calendar (Saka calendar) is the official calendar of India. It starts from the Saka era which begins on March 22, 78 CE (or March 21 in leap years).

2. **Import necessary libraries**: We need the `datetime` module to work with dates.

3. **Define the conversion logic**: 
   - The Saka year = Gregorian year - 78 (when the date is after March 21/22)
   - If the date is before March 21/22, then Saka year = Gregorian year - 79
   - Convert the day of year to appropriate Saka month and day

4. **Handle leap years**: In leap years, the Saka calendar starts on March 21, otherwise on March 22.

5. **Calculate Saka month and day**: Based on the day of year from the Saka new year, determine which Saka month and day it corresponds to.

6. **Return the result**: Return the Saka year, month, and day as integers.

```python
from datetime import datetime, date

def gregorian_to_saka_calendar(gregorian_date: date) -> tuple:
    """
    Convert a Gregorian date to Indian National Calendar (Saka) date.
    Returns (saka_year, saka_month, saka_day) as integers.
    """
    year = gregorian_date.year
    
    # Determine if it's a leap year
    is_leap = (year % 4 == 0 and year % 100 != 0) or (year % 400 == 0)
    
    # Saka new year starts on March 21 (leap year) or March 22 (non-leap year)
    saka_new_year_month = 3
    saka_new_year_day = 21 if is_leap else 22
    
    # Create the Saka new year date for the current Gregorian year
    saka_new_year = date(year, saka_new_year_month, saka_new_year_day)
    
    # Check if the given date is before or after Saka new year
    if gregorian_date >= saka_new_year:
        saka_year = year - 78
        days_from_saka_new_year = (gregorian_date - saka_new_year).days + 1
    else:
        saka_year = year - 79
        # Calculate from previous year's Saka new year
        prev_year_leap = ((year-1) % 4 == 0 and (year-1) % 100 != 0) or ((year-1) % 400 == 0)
        prev_saka_new_year_day = 21 if prev_year_leap else 22
        prev_saka_new_year = date(year-1, 3, prev_saka_new_year_day)
        days_from_saka_new_year = (gregorian_date - prev_saka_new_year).days + 1
    
    # Saka months and their days
    # First 6 months have 31 days (except Chaitra in non-leap years has 30)
    # Last 6 months have 30 days each
    saka_month_days = [31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 30, 30, 30, 30, 30, 30]
    
    # Adjust first month for non-leap years
    if not is_leap:
        saka_month_days[0] = 30
    
    # Find the Saka month and day
    saka_month = 1
    remaining_days = days_from_saka_new_year
    
    for i, month_days in enumerate(saka_month_days):
        if remaining_days <= month_days:
            saka_month = i + 1
            saka_day = remaining_days
            break
        remaining_days -= month_days
    
    return saka_year, saka_month, saka_day
```
