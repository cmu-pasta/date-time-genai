Let's think step by step to solve this problem.
1. Import the pendulum library to work with Pendulum date/time types.
2. Define a function that accepts a pendulum.DateTime representing the calendar date.
3. Assume the fiscal year starts on April 1 and is labeled by the calendar year in which it ends.
4. If the month is April (4) or later, the fiscal year is the date's year plus one; otherwise, it's the date's year.
5. Return the fiscal year as an integer.

```python
import pendulum

def determine_fiscal_year(dt: pendulum.DateTime) -> int:
    """
    Determine the fiscal year for a given date, assuming the fiscal year starts on April 1
    and is labeled by the calendar year in which it ends.

    Examples:
    - 2023-03-31 -> FY 2023
    - 2023-04-01 -> FY 2024
    """
    return dt.year + 1 if dt.month >= 4 else dt.year
```
